Read the transcript

AI chats forget. Your work shouldn't. StatePort gives AI-assisted work a durable home on your own machine.

A real app retains its context: the study plan, the progress, the evidence — not a blank chat box.

Consequential changes are reviewed before they land — and they stay undoable.

And every run leaves a receipt: what was asked, what was allowed, what was done.

StatePort Alpha.16 is published, but a fresh installation failed its signature check. A corrected release is being prepared; read the current status before installing.

Why it matters

Three things stay true.

01

Your work stays yours

Goals, decisions, and history live in readable files on your own machine — not in a session locked inside someone else's chat.

02

Continuity is designed for restarts

Goals, decisions, and history live in durable files so you can pick up where you left off.

03

Change stays reviewable

Important changes are proposed first. You can inspect the result and see what happened.

Current availability

Alpha.16 fresh installation is blocked.

Alpha.16 remains published, but fresh installation is blocked by a known signature-check failure. Anonymous image downloads also fail with HTTP 401 because the release image registry packages are private. Fixing the signature check alone will not complete installation. Wait for a corrected, qualified release; repeating this installer does not repair either failure. Published files remain available for inspection.

StatePort is in early alpha. Do not use it for important data.

The release includes the web interface, API, worker, persistence, exact rootless container runtime, and isolated execution host. Its intended workflow includes StudyState and importing ProjectState, StudyState, and StateSpec Git repositories; the fresh installation failure prevents recommending this path today. Exact signed artifacts and qualification categories remain visible on the release page.

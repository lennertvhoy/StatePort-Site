What is StatePort?

StatePort keeps the goals, progress and decisions of AI-assisted work in readable files on your machine, not in one chat session.

An AI tool can help with the work, but it does not own your data or decide its own permissions. In the StudyState sample, StatePort keeps the saved state, shows the proposed change and records the result. Output from coding agents is recorded as a run result and is not saved into the app’s state.

What is StatePort?

StatePort gives AI-assisted applications a durable place to keep goals, progress, decisions, and history. That information lives in readable files on your machine instead of being trapped in one chat session.

Applications can ask an AI tool to help with work, but the tool does not own your data or decide its own permissions. StatePort keeps the current state, shows important proposed changes, and records the result.

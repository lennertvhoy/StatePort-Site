What you will see

  1. Where work starts. Most AI tools start with an empty chat. StatePort starts with an app for work you want to continue.
  2. What stays together. Your goal, files, decisions, history, and AI conversations stay together.
  3. What you can ask. You can ask an AI coding helper a normal question, or let it prepare useful work.
  4. Where code runs. When the AI runs code, StatePort gives it a sealed-off container instead of unlimited access to your computer.
  5. What you review. Routine work can keep moving. Important changes stay visible for you to review.
  6. What happens when you return. Close it. Return later. The app still knows what you were doing.
  7. The point. StatePort gives ongoing AI work a home you can inspect, control, and keep.

How to read the claims

The video shows a working public-safe fixture and its recorded UI results. It does not turn that fixture into a downloadable software release; the release ledger and proof and roadmap keep those claims separate.

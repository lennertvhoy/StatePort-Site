Read the transcript

AI chats forget. Your work shouldn't. StatePort gives AI-assisted work a durable home on your own machine.

A real app retains its context: the study plan, the progress, the evidence — not a blank chat box.

And every run leaves a receipt: what was asked, what was allowed, what was done.

What you will see

  1. Your work has a home. StatePort keeps app data on your computer.
  2. The application keeps its state in files. StudyState keeps the goal, plan, and progress together.
  3. You review important changes. The app shows a proposal before it changes saved work.
  4. You can see what happened. Each run leaves a change record.
  5. The closing status is out of date. The video ends with a status from an early alpha. See the release page for the current one.

What this video is

This is a recording of the interface with sample data, made in August 2026. It shows the intended experience. Where it differs from Alpha.20 (the undo line above, for example), this page and current limitations are right and the video is not. Current limitations lists what has been measured on Alpha.20. The release status and the roadmap say what comes next.

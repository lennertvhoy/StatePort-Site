What StudyState is

StudyState is a study coach built around your goals, activities, progress, and reflections. The conversation belongs to the application, and the useful facts live in files you can open.

Most of the time, you see the coach and your next task. History and change records are available when you need them.

The Focus default: what to do next, and why

The default view answers one question: what should I do next, and why? StudyState plans the next learning activity from your durable learning state, so the suggestion is grounded in your goals and history rather than in whatever happened to be said last in a chat.

Lifecycle you control

The design lets you start, pause, resume, or change an activity. The coach suggests the next step. You stay in control of the plan.

Reflection becomes a change you review

When you reflect on what you learned, StudyState can turn that reflection into a proposed update. You review it before it changes your saved progress.

Undo and change history

The preview shows the planned ability to undo an applied change and review its history. This work is still part of the product roadmap.

State that survives restarts

Your goals, activities, and progress are designed to live in durable files on your machine, so a new session does not start from nothing.

What StudyState deliberately does not do

  • The current preview uses sample activity. It is not a live tutoring service.
  • It does not change your learning state silently. Mutations are proposed, reviewed, and explicitly applied.
  • Importing an existing learning history is not available yet.
  • StudyState is an early-alpha application, not a finished or independently evaluated tutoring service.

Use StudyState

  1. Install and open StatePort.
  2. Open Catalog and install the reviewed StudyState sample, or put your StudyState Git repository below /var/lib/stateport/imports.
  3. For your own repository, choose Import a local repository, review the exact commit and detected StudyState adapter, approve it, and create the isolated copy.
  4. Open the application. Goals, activity, proposals, approvals, receipts, and history remain attached to that durable instance.

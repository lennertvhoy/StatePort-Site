What this means

Published and installable are separate states:

  • Signed and online — yes. The Alpha.5 artifacts are immutable and the public image references resolve anonymously.
  • Installation path enabled — no.
  • Clean install verified — no.
  • Human accepted — not yet. Alpha, still being reviewed.
  • Security review — not done.
  • Production-ready — no.

Historical releases remain unchanged. v0.1.0-alpha.2 is superseded and disabled. v0.1.0-alpha.3 is signed and byte-intact but install-disabled; its erratum remains the historical authority. Neither tree was changed to publish Alpha.5.

What the corrected successor must prove

The next candidate starts from fresh evidence. Alpha.2’s publication record cannot be transferred to changed product bytes.

  1. Exact sourceA frozen canonical source head and normal-clone-resolvable public snapshot identity.
  2. Complete web imageThe packaged runtime imports the updater and preview gateway and returns its health response.
  3. Fresh supply chainRebuilt images, SBOMs, scans, provenance, signatures, and signed index.
  4. Verified install pathA clean public one-command install on every claimed host baseline.
  5. Acceptance recordRestart/reread evidence and an explicit owner verdict on the exact successor.

Why the language is strict Signed and published does not mean installable, accepted, reviewed, or production-ready. The known failure remains visible rather than being hidden behind a new label.

What this means

Published means the signed files are online and the installer works on a matching host. It does not mean reviewed, accepted, or production-ready. These are different things, and we keep them separate:

  • Signed and online — yes. The installer downloads signed images and checks every signature.
  • Clean install verified — Ubuntu 24.04 yes; Fedora 44 works but is not fully tested.
  • Human accepted — not yet. Alpha, still being reviewed.
  • Security review — not done.
  • Production-ready — no.

About alpha.2. v0.1.0-alpha.2 is superseded and disabled — use alpha.3. Its signed files stay online for inspection and were not changed.

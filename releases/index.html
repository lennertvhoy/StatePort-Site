What this means

Published means the signed files are online and byte-intact. It does not mean installable, reviewed, accepted, or production-ready. These are different things, and we keep them separate:

  • Signed and online — yes. The signed artifacts are unchanged since publication.
  • Currently installable — no. Signed freshness evidence has expired and known installer and runtime defects require a successor release. Do not run the old one-line install command.
  • Clean install verified — historical local receipts for Ubuntu 24.04 and Fedora 44 only; they are not current install proof.
  • Human accepted — not yet. Alpha, still being reviewed.
  • Security review — not done.
  • Production-ready — no.

About alpha.2. v0.1.0-alpha.2 is superseded and disabled. Its signed files stay online for inspection and were not changed.

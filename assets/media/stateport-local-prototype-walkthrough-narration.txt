Hi! This is StatePort — a place on your own computer where the work you do
with AI can live. Let me show you what that means, in about a minute.

This is one of those projects. Instead of hiding inside a chat, it keeps its
own notes: what the goal is, what happened so far, and what needs your
attention next.

You can still chat with an AI helper — here it is called a coding agent.
StatePort runs it through a managed workspace on your machine. A job can use a
sandbox or container when you configure one. The agent gets bounded tools and
permissions, and asks before anything important changes.

This page is the project's memory: what is finished, what is still being
checked, and what is only an experiment — so you know what to trust.

And yes — the same project reads fine on your phone.

One honest note: this is a preview from my own computer. StatePort is in
private alpha, works with one AI coding tool so far, and can't be downloaded
yet. The idea is simple: give AI work a real home, keep you in charge, and
keep what's yours, yours.

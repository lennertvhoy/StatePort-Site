Design your first durable application.

Move the important facts of a small workflow out of a temporary chat and into an application you can understand later.

Design exercise. This uses an imaginary study planner. You will not install anything.

New here? This is a thinking exercise, not a setup guide — you will not install anything. We imagine a small study planner and ask how its important facts can live outside a chat window, in plain language. If a word ever feels technical, it is safe to keep reading: the idea matters more than the vocabulary.

The scenario

Imagine a study planner that helps someone prepare for an exam. A chat assistant could suggest a plan, but the learner still needs to see the current plan, upcoming work, decisions, and completed evidence after the chat ends.

The goal is not to make the assistant cleverer. The goal is to make the application easy to understand and continue later.

1. Separate facts from conversation

Write down the facts that must survive a new model, a shorter chat history, or an offline review:

  • the learner’s goal and target date;
  • the current plan and approved changes;
  • a record that a task was completed or checked;
  • the rules for who may make changes.

A conversation can discuss these facts. It should not be the only copy of them.

2. Give every file an owner

Separate reusable application files, personal files, and generated output. Then you can tell whether the AI tool changed something it was allowed to change.

3. Model a small lifecycle

For one proposed plan adjustment, the lifecycle could be as simple as:

proposal ↓ review and approval ↓ change ↓ check ↓ record and current state

Each step has a different meaning. A change may be approved but fail its checks. Keeping the steps separate makes that failure visible.

4. Keep the assistant in its role

The assistant may propose a plan, explain a change, or prepare a draft. It does not receive permission just because it suggested the idea. If the tool cannot do something safely, it should stop and tell you.

5. End with a clear result

A useful result is more than "done." Keep the current plan, what changed, whether the checks passed, and what still needs attention.

Takeaway

Start with the facts, ownership, permissions, steps, and change history. AI assistance becomes more useful when it works inside those boundaries.

StudyState Sample: review an activity, save a reflection, and return to your progress.

Read the full transcript

In this sample learning application, the goal and next activity are saved with the work.

Starting an activity first opens a review. It names the activity and shows the exact change, from not started to in progress. Approve only when it matches your intent.

After approval, StatePort applies the change and updates the learning state. The activity is now in progress. You can pause it, choose another activity, or write a reflection.

A reflection starts as a browser draft. Review its exact effect before saving it as evidence. StatePort keeps self-reported learning separate from independently verified progress.

Open the receipt to inspect what StatePort applied. The recorded result belongs to this application. It provides a trail you can inspect, without treating a saved reflection as independently verified learning.

After restarting the local service, the completed activity and reflection are still here.
